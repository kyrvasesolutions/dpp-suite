Reading this sheet
PCF-1: the footprint result ID, the identifier the laboratory file gives to one calculated footprint result.
scope: the customer-scoped alias. It names the customer whose own article number is shown next to it.
en/construction/EU: a qualifier of a text. It gives the language, the sector and the market the text is approved for.
Rule DR-TENSILE-LIMITS: a rule ID. It names the rule in the pinned contract pack that made a derived statement, which says whether a value lies
within limits; the value itself is not shown.
The record digest and the contract pack digest in the footer identify exactly which record and which contract this sheet was made from.
